The bridge lane

ChainStoredIn effectSet to

Unset is not off — it means the page decides, which today leaves the choice to each viewer: on Ethereum the lane is always shown (its bridges need no relayer), and on an L2 the viewer sees a door they can open themselves. On shows the lane to everyone with no door to find — use it once a relayer is actually running. Off withdraws it from everyone including viewers who already opted in, which is the only remedy an immutable page has if the thing behind a lane breaks. The page caches an Off, so a node that later refuses to answer cannot undo it.

All chains is the fallback: a chain with its own entry ignores it, so you can turn everything off and bring one chain back up.

Stewardship

Two steps: proposing changes nothing until the named address accepts. Propose the zero address to cancel an offer. Getting this wrong is the one thing the contract cannot recover from — a lane nobody can ever withdraw again.
