Every order is an NFT, and the art is on chain

Three order types, for tokens and for NFTs. Placing one mints an ERC-721 whose image is drawn by an immutable per-board renderer — no server, no IPFS, nothing to expire. Every card below is real tokenURI output from the deployed boards.

Fixed limit
Sell 1 WBTC for 95,000 USDC

A price you name, and it rests there until someone fills it or you cancel. This one is all-or-nothing and expires in 7 days — both your choice.

Fixed limit · partial
Sell 4,000 USDC for 1 WETH

The same order type, marked fillable in pieces. A taker can clear part of it and leave the rest resting at the same price.

Dutch decay
250,000 ZORG · 0.40 → 0.10 WETH

Starts high and falls to a floor you set, over a window you set — 24h here. It then rests at that floor until you cancel, or until an end you give it.

Climbing bid
Buy 100,000 USDC · 0.0245 → 0.0262 WETH

A Dutch in reverse: your bid rises toward a ceiling you name, over a window you choose. 12h in this one.

Dutch decay · NFT
Milady #7 · 6 → 3 WETH

The same decay, selling one NFT instead of a token balance. Priced down over whatever window you pick.

Climbing bid · NFT floor
Any Milady · 2.2 → 3.4 WETH

No token id named, so it takes any one from the collection — a floor bid. Name ids instead and it narrows to those.

Escrowed on chain when placed, so a fill cannot fail for want of funds · cancelling returns the escrow · every price, window and expiry above is the maker’s choice, not a board setting
