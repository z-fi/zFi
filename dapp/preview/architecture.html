zSwap runs on Ethereum. Not on a server that talks to Ethereum.

The page itself is contract bytecode. Ten data contracts hold the HTML; an eleventh reassembles and serves it over ERC-8244 and ERC-5219. There are zero build steps between the source and what you load — no host to go down, no domain to seize, and no version anyone can change, us included.
