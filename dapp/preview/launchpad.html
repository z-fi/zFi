A trading pool that starts with no ETH in it

One transaction mints the token and opens its market. You supply no ether, only gas — the pool opens holding your token and nothing else. Ether arrives when people buy, and it cannot leave: the LP is locked in the contract forever, which is what gives holders a floor to redeem against.

At launch

Every token goes in. You pick the starting market cap, and that alone sets the opening price.

As people buy

Ether only enters by purchase. It stays: the LP can never be pulled, by anyone.

The floor

Burning tokens to take ether back. Supply only ever falls, so the floor never falls for anyone else.
