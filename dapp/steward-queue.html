zSteward — the delay on zSwap's lists

zSteward at is an optional owner for the lists zSwap reads its nodes, relays and aggregators from. zSwap follows the lists whoever holds them; a list held here takes any addition only after a few days in public, during which the guardian can cancel it. Removing an entry, disabling a solver or switching a lane off applies at once. Everything on this page is read from chain; no wallet is needed to see what is queued.

Queue or remove

The steward queues; the steward or the guardian removes. Calldata that only removes an entry, disables a solver or switches a lane off goes through narrow and applies at once; anything else is queued. The list admin pages route their writes here on their own once zSteward owns the list.
