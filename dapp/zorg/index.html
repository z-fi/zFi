Escrow & receipt

Bonding escrows one zOrgz, stated zOrg amount, and an ETH loyalty principal, then mints a transferable receipt with the same ID. Tier commitments begin immediately; adding zOrg renews the commitment and ETH maturity. Mature exits return ETH in full. Early exits split a tax between Moloch and remaining bonded receipts. Eternalization requires 10,000 zOrg and permanently escrows the zOrgz and zOrg.
