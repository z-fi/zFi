Why the order decides the quote

The dapp merges this list ahead of its built-in seeds and reads it once per session, so index 0 is what a visitor without a wallet quotes against. A node with a low eth_call ceiling still answers single-hop and split A but returns nothing for routed, 3-hop and split B — and because Multicall3 reports that as a successful call carrying a null result, the page never falls through to another node and never warns.
